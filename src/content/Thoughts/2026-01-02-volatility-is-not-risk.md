### Market snapshot
- Index action: selective, low follow-through
- Volatility: compressed, but not “safe”
- Leadership: narrow participation

### Insight
Volatility is a price of optionality, not a synonym for danger.  
Risk hides in positioning, liquidity, and correlation when regimes shift.  
In quiet markets, the cost is not movement — it’s complacency.

### Question
What would break first if the market moved *slowly* against consensus?
